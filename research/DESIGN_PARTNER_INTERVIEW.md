# Continuum design-partner interview

Target: three AI platform, security, governance, SRE, or agent-infrastructure practitioners. Do not recruit only friends who will praise the demo.

## Five questions

1. Tell me about the last time an agent used stale, conflicting, cross-tenant, or unauthorized context. What actually happened?
2. Which agent actions in your environment are irreversible enough to justify a memory admission gate?
3. What would prevent you from placing Continuum in Shadow mode for one workflow next week?
4. Which receipt fields would your incident responder, auditor, or security engineer need after a bad action?
5. What is the strongest reason this product should not exist or would fail inside your stack?

## Evidence rule

Record role—not name or employer unless explicitly approved—date, workflow, sharpest objection, resulting product decision, and whether one anonymous quote may be published. Do not convert praise into evidence; prioritize objections that caused a product change.

## Outreach message

> I’m testing Continuum, an open-source memory firewall that evaluates agent memories before high-impact actions. I’m looking for a 15-minute adversarial critique from someone working with AI platforms, security, SRE, governance, or agent infrastructure. I’m specifically looking for reasons it would fail—not compliments. With your permission, I may publish one anonymous role-level quote and the product change it caused.
